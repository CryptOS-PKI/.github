# Trademarks

CryptOS-PKI™, FleetOS™ and their logos are unregistered (common-law) trademarks of the
rabbit hole tech, llc. They're not registered yet, which is why you'll see ™ rather than ®. Applications
to register them are being prepared, so full registered protection is pending;
common-law rights already apply from first use.

## What's fine, no permission needed

- Referring to the project, or any of its components, by name in your own writing,
  talks, docs or code.
- Saying your product or integration "works with CryptOS" or "is based on CryptOS".
- Redistributing the project unmodified, under its own name.

## What needs permission

- Running CryptOS, or Fleet Manager, as a hosted or SaaS offering under the CryptOS
  or FleetOS name.
- Using "CryptOS" or "FleetOS" (or anything confusingly close to either) as the name
  of your own product or company.
- Distributing a modified fork under the CryptOS or FleetOS name. A fork needs a name
  of its own.

## Logos

The CryptOS and FleetOS logos may be used unmodified, and only to refer to the
project itself. They may not be altered, combined with another mark, or used in a
way that implies endorsement.
